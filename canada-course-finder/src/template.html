How it works

From shortlist to study permit

The usual order of things for an applicant from Bangladesh. Your counsellor walks you through each step.

  1. Shortlist three to six programs

    Use the finder to match your IELTS, budget and intake. Mix a few strong fits with one or two stretch options.

  2. Check them with a counsellor

    They compare your academic results with each program's entry requirement and confirm the current fees and scholarships.

  3. Apply to the universities

    Each application has its own fee, usually about CAD 100 to 150. Send transcripts, test scores and any statement the program asks for.

  4. Accept an offer and pay the deposit

    Deposits are listed on each program. Keep the receipt, you need it for the visa.

  5. Get your provincial attestation letter

    Most new students need one from the province. The university requests it for you after you accept the offer. Your counsellor confirms if your program needs it.

  6. Apply for the study permit

    Medical test, police clearance and proof of funds go in with the application. See the costs below.
